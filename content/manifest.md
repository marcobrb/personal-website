### Free Information
Access to quality information is how we shape, improve, and flourish as humans. If you discover something and you make it easily accessible, anyone can benefit from it (and by return, you'll benefit from the discoveries of others). Free information is also a great driver of equality.

### Think Probabilistically
Much about life comes down to luck. Live life like luck doesn't exist, but when you have to think about how one life goes, keep it in mind.

### Be Curious 
Learn to say "It is curious that ..." whenever it feels like it. It is a powerful way to make you think about things in a creative way.
